Defying the Odds Fitness uses fitness as a vehicle for confidence, self-discipline, health and long-term life skills.

Defying the Odds Fitness

8 main objectives.

The program is designed to strengthen physical health while building the behaviors, habits and skills young people can carry into the rest of their lives.

1

To increase the frequency and duration of exercise habits for participating youth.

2

To increase other positive health habits such as dietary practices.

3

To increase fitness levels in aerobic capacity, strength, flexibility and body composition.

4

To increase self esteem.

5

To increase behavioral control “life skills” applied to maintain a fitness program to include self assessment, goal setting and planning skills.

6

To increase behaviors representative of the values of respect, responsibility and self-discipline.

7

To increase the ability to generalize the “life skills” learned through physical activity for dealing with problem areas such as substance abuse, violent behavior, delinquency and school performance.

8

To decrease the incidence of negative behaviors such as substance abuse, violent behavior, criminal behavior and academic problems.
