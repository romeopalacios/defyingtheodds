Workforce Development
Program.

A Defying the Odds Fitness / ISSA partnership.

Fitness education that creates opportunity.

Defying The Odds Fitness and ISSA have teamed up to create a transformative workforce development program that not only provides high-quality education in fitness training but also empowers individuals to become experts in fitness while addressing social issues and promoting personal well-being.

Program Focus

  • The program offers a holistic approach to fitness training, combining state-of-the-art strength training, nutritional health promotion, and positive reinforcement.
  • Participants are provided with case management and career support to guide them through their personal and professional development in and beyond personal training.
  • The program aims to elevate individuals as experts in fitness, drawing upon their lived experiences to address social issues and promote positive change.
  • Through the program, participants are encouraged to take self-responsibility for their well-being, leading to a lifetime of improved personal and professional growth.

Benefits

  • Participants receive comprehensive training in fitness and personal development, equipping them with the skills and knowledge to thrive in the fitness industry.
  • The program provides a platform for individuals to share their stories and expertise, empowering them to make a positive impact in their communities.
  • By addressing social issues and promoting self-responsibility, the program creates a pathway for long-term well-being and success for program participants.

Overall, the workforce development program between Defying The Odds Fitness and ISSA goes beyond traditional fitness training by empowering individuals to become agents of change in their communities. Through a combination of expert fitness training, personal development support, and a focus on social issues, the program aims to cultivate a new generation of fitness professionals committed to making a difference in the world.
